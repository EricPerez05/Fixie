In New Jersey, glass containers are a required recyclable in every county. Empty and rinse the jar, take off the metal lid and recycle the lid with metal cans, and put the jar loose in the bin, not in a plastic bag. Drinking glasses, cookware, mirrors and window glass are not glass containers and do not go in the bin.
