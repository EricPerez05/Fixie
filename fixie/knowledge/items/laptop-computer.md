New Jersey's Electronic Waste Management Act bans putting computers in the trash. Take it to a free e-waste collection site: a county or municipal drop-off or a participating retailer (NJ DEP publishes a yearly list of sites). Back up and wipe your data first. If the battery is swollen, don't charge or press on it.
