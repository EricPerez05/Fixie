Most curbside programs can't separate the thin plastic lining from the paper, so the cup usually goes in the trash. A few cities and some coffee chains do accept them; check your hauler. The plastic lid is often a different plastic: check its number against your local list. A cardboard sleeve can go in paper recycling.
