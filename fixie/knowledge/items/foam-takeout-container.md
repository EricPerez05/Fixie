No New Jersey county lists polystyrene foam as a required recyclable, so foam food containers go in the trash. Do not put them in the recycling bin. Since May 4, 2022, New Jersey food businesses may not sell or serve food in polystyrene foam containers, cups or plates, apart from a few narrow exemptions.
