New Jersey's e-waste law bans televisions from the trash. Take it to a free e-waste collection site (a county or municipal drop-off, or a participating retailer); sites under a manufacturer plan may not charge you. For TVs over 40 inches or devices of 50 pounds or more, NJ DEP lists recyclers that offer home pick-up.
