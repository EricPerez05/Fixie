Phones are not covered by New Jersey's e-waste disposal ban, but NJ DEP says their rechargeable batteries should be recycled, never put in the trash or recycling bin. Take the phone to a retailer or carrier take-back program, a Call2Recycle drop-off, or your county's household hazardous waste program. Wipe your data first. If the battery is swollen, don't charge or press on it.
