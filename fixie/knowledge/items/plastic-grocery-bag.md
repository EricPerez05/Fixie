Do not put plastic bags in the recycling bin, and never bag your recyclables: bags jam sorting machines (Newark lists plastic grocery bags as not accepted). Since May 4, 2022, New Jersey stores and food businesses may not provide single-use plastic carryout bags, and grocery stores of 2,500 sq ft or more may only offer reusable bags. Reuse any bags you already have, then put them in the trash.
