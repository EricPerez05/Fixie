In New Jersey, #1 (PET) and #2 (HDPE) plastic bottles and jugs are a required recyclable in 20 of 21 counties; Gloucester County does not mandate them. Empty and rinse the bottle and put it loose in the bin, not in a plastic bag. Follow your town's rule on caps. Other plastics (#3 to #7) vary by town.
