In New Jersey, ordinary alkaline batteries can go in the regular trash: NJ DEP says they now fall below hazardous waste standards. Check the label first. If it says rechargeable, Ni-MH, Ni-Cd or lithium, it is not alkaline and must be recycled instead. Do not put any battery in the recycling bin.
