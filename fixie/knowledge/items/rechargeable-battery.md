NJ DEP says to recycle all rechargeable batteries (Ni-Cd, Ni-MH, Li-ion, Ni-Zn and small sealed lead under 11 lbs). Never put them in the trash or recycling bin: damaged lithium batteries start fires in collection trucks. Tape over the terminals or bag each battery on its own, then take it to a retail drop-off (find one at call2recycle.org) or your county's household hazardous waste program. If it is swollen, hot or leaking, keep it away from anything flammable.
