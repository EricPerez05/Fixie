New Jersey's e-waste law covers desktop printers and fax machines, so they may not go in the trash. Take it to a free e-waste collection site. Remove the ink or toner cartridges first: they do not go in curbside recycling (Newark lists ink cartridges as not accepted), but many office-supply stores take them back.
