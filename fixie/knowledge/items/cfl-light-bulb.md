Fluorescent bulbs contain mercury, and NJ DEP says mercury-containing items should be disposed of properly, not in the trash or recycling bin. Take them to your county's household hazardous waste program or a retailer that takes bulbs back; some NJ counties also list fluorescent bulbs as a designated recyclable. If one breaks, air out the room, don't vacuum, and seal the pieces in a jar or bag for drop-off.
