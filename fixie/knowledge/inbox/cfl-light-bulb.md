Fluorescent bulbs contain a small amount of mercury, so keep them out of the trash and household recycling. Take them to a bulb drop-off, such as many hardware stores or a household hazardous waste site. If one breaks, air out the room, don't vacuum the pieces, and seal them in a jar or bag for drop-off.
