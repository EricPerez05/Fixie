Never put it in household recycling or trash: damaged lithium-ion batteries start fires in collection trucks and sorting plants. Tape over the terminals or bag it on its own, then take it to a battery drop-off point, such as an electronics or hardware store, or a household hazardous waste site. If it is swollen, hot or leaking, keep it away from anything flammable and ask your hazardous waste site how to bring it in.
